MUMBAI

Mumbai is the financial capital of India. It is located on the western coast along the Arabian Sea. The city was formerly known as Bombay. Mumbai is famous for the Gateway of India.
It is home to Bollywood, India’s film industry. The city has the busiest railway network in the country. Marine Drive is called the “Queen’s Necklace” at night. Mumbai houses the famous Siddhivinayak Temple.
It is also known for the Elephanta Caves, a UNESCO site. The city never sleeps and is always full of energy. Mumbai is a melting pot of cultures and traditions. It is the largest city in India by population.

Mumbai is the largest city in India. It lies on the western coast along the Arabian Sea. The city was earlier called Bombay. It is the financial hub of the country. Bollywood, India’s film industry, is based here. The Gateway of India is a famous landmark. Marine Drive shines like a necklace at night. Mumbai has the busiest suburban railway system.

The Elephanta Caves are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Siddhivinayak Temple is a popular place of worship. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus is a historic railway station. Street food like vada pav and pav bhaji are loved by all. The city is known as “the city that never sleeps.” People from many cultures live together here. Mumbai is also home to India’s biggest stock exchange. It is a city of dreams, energy, and endless opportunities.
